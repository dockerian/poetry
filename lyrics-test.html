非打油者，「夢油」是也

无眠的灵魂 在四处游荡 寂静的冬夜里 传来一声门响 黑暗的恐惧 堆积莫名的紧张 心中的怪想 仿佛最後的抵抗 借着微弱灯光 我迷朦地张望 轻轻走过的 原来是俺娘

Night Mystery (2008) ⮃ 

A sleepless soul in the night of winter Stillness in stillness dream came out to wander The dark was deeper the dread was bigger As puzzle built up fever nightmare seized thriller Shadow of a feather sunk heart like a hammer Gently into the picture - it was my dear mother
Conjugal doggerel series, by Divertida LZ

两只老虎

儿歌版

两只老虎，两只老虎， 跑得快，跑得快， 一只没有耳朵（眼睛），一只没有尾巴， 真奇怪！真奇怪！

附原版 (Frère Jacques)

睡眼朦胧，睡眼朦胧 约翰兄，约翰兄？ 外面响起晨钟，外面响起晨钟 叮、铛、咚！叮、铛、咚！
2008.02.07.

Do You Hear (2008) ⮃ 

+ English Translation

There're two tigers, there're two tigers Ran like deers, ran like deers One had no tail, one had no ear It's so weird, it's so queer

+ My English version

Do you cheer, do you hear? I am here, I am near Do you like to stare? Don't you have to fear? Oh my deer, oh my dear
Lyrics by Jason Zhu

一日不見

彼采葛兮。 一日不见，如三月兮。 彼采萧兮。 一日不见，如三秋兮。 彼采艾兮。 一日不见，如三岁兮。
原作出自《诗经•王风•采葛》

One Day No See (2008) ⮃ 

One month no mail, No bail in jail One week no see, Far from the sea One day no call, Long as one fall And far and long, I sing this song As far as long, I send this poem
英译：舟山 2008.04.15.
