### 糊涂的爱｜The Puzzle Of Love <a name="hutudeai"></a>

  ```
  爱有几分能说清楚　　　　　　　How much is love? No one is sure
  还有几分是糊里又糊涂　　　　　Drifting in doubt, not knowing what to do
  情有几分是温存　　　　　　　　How much of feeling reveals what is true?
  还有几分是涩涩的酸楚　　　　　Drifting of heart, in lost and in blue

  忘不掉的一幕一幕　　　　　　　There is a dream we're longing to pursue
  却留不住往日的温度　　　　　　No more to hold the warmth that I used to
  意念中的热热乎乎　　　　　　　There's a time to know who in love with who
  是真是假、是甜还是苦　　　　　When all begins, and we can see it through

  这就是爱、说也说不清楚　　　　That is the love, a puzzle unresolved-issue
  这就是爱、糊里又糊涂　　　　　That is the love, with no remedy to cue
  这就是爱、牠忘记了人间的烦恼　That is the love, to make life pure and new
  这就是爱、能保持着糊涂的温度　That is the love, to keep it only for you

  这就是爱、她有幻想无数　　　　This is the love, your beauty in my view
  这就是爱、是真情的流露　　　　This is the love, for a long time since I knew
  这就是爱、她带给你神秘的礼物　This is the love, the love you give me too
  这就是爱、是我为你写下的祝福　This is the love, the love only for you
  ```

### 我从草原来｜Coming From The Prairie <a name="wocongcaoyuanlai"></a>

  ```
  我立马千山外，听风唱着天籁　Standing my horse high, I join the singing from sky
  岁月已经更改，心胸依然自在　Time changes and flies, my dreams never die
  我放歌万里外，明月与我同在　Singing out a love song, I have the moon up and bright
  远方为我等待，心澎湃　　　　Your waiting afar for me, my heart stirs, with delight

  我寻梦梦就在，未来为我盛开　My dreams coming true, future is all I can do
  天空开始泛白，脚步如此轻快　I enjoy a morning walk, while dawn light breakthrough
  我想爱爱就来，不要寂寞尘埃　My love coming to find me, I leave my sadness behind
  心里花开不败，才精彩　　　　Flowers blooming in heart, in a beautiful mind

  风从草原来，吹动我心怀　　　Coming from the prairie, the wind stirs my heart
  吹来我的爱，这花香的海　　　Bringing the scent of flowers, the sea of love of art
  我从草原来，温暖你心怀　　　Coming from the prairie, the wind can warm your heart
  不变我的情，那天蓝的爱　　　Bringing my love, never changed, and never be apart

  雲白，白出了毡房华盖　　　　Clouds white, as Mongolian yurts in sight
  草绿，绿出了绵延如海　　　　Grass green, as a sea, as a widescreen
  花红，红出了急风烈马　　　　Flowers red, fiery steeds are leading ahead
  ```

### 海闊天空｜Under A Vast Sky <a name="haikuotiankong"></a>

  ```
  今天我、寒夜里看雪飘过　　　The cold night snow is drifting through the silent glow
  怀著冷却了的心窝飘远方　　　With a frozen heart, I've walked this road so long
  风雨里追赶、雾里分不清影踪　In the wind and rain, In the mist, I've nowhere to belong
  天空海阔你与我、可会变　　　The sky and sea, where vast things end — still we remain

  多少次、迎着冷眼与嘲笑　　　All those years, all the ridicule that I knew
  从没有放弃过心中的理想　　　Never gave up on the dream I held so strong
  一刹那恍惚、若有所失的感觉　In a fleeting haze, I lost my mind and my call
  不知不觉已变淡、心里爱　　　Unaware, the love fades like sand — I can't explain

  原谅我这一生不羁放纵爱自由　Forgive me, I chose this life and let it flow
  也会怕有一天会跌倒　　　　　Someday, there's a weakness I'll have to show
  背弃了理想、谁人都可以　　　To desert a dream, it's so easy to do
  那会怕有一天、只你共我　　　As long as the world has me and you

  仍然自由自我　　　　　　　　I hold on tight to all love endows
  永远高唱我歌　　　　　　　　Forever singing out my vow
  走遍千里　　　　　　　　　　A long way to go
  ```

### 一笑江湖｜A Knight's Wild Heart <a name="yixiaojianghu"></a>

  ```
  江湖一笑浪滔滔，紅塵盡忘了　The river surges, and laughter echoes bold
  俱往矣何足言道　　　　　　　The past is gone, and (mortal) dust grows old
  蒼天一笑笑不老，豪情卻會了　All (my) valiant pride, in a lonely tale untold
  對月飲一杯寂寥　　　　　　　At smiles, a cup of (solace) wine to hold

  劍起江湖恩怨，拂袖罩明月　　The sword awakes to vengeance and to grace
  西風葉落花謝，枕刀劍難眠　　The sighs of years across the rest and chase
  汝為山河過客　　　　　　　　A passer-by this world
  卻總長嘆傷離別　　　　　　　yet finds within no peace
  鬢如霜一杯濃烈　　　　　　　A drink (of frost) is strong, yet none shall cease

  隻身走過多少的歲月　　　　　Alone I walk through countless years
  看慣刀光照亮過黑夜　　　　　In the light of blades to face the fears
  俠骨魔心如何來分辨　　　　　Where lies the line, it's not so clear
  彈指一夢不過一瞬間　　　　　Off dreams the moment, I'm still here
  ```

### 人间道｜The Rightful World <a name="renjiandao"></a>

  ```
  我要走穿这条命，去看雪兰花　I want to use this life, in search of wild freesia
  我要踏破这双鞋，光脚平风沙　I want to use this hand to make a panacea
  我要白日见雲霞、夜里举火把　I want a sky without dark, under bright-lit stars
  我要这朗朗乾坤下事事有王法　I want a lawful clean of yard with all it regards

  苦雨江头风波恶　　　　　　　The bitter rain, the river's roar
  做人一生坎坷多　　　　　　　The rough is life, I've weathered more
  兄弟我心头上有件事儿啊　　　There's one last weighing carried evermore
  不见孟婆不得过　　　　　　　No light shall guide me past that door
  自古黑白分颜色　　　　　　　The world of shade has carved a line to draw
  别扯放下就成佛　　　　　　　Don't chant that peace absolves the flaw
  好人都未必能有好报啊　　　　The good may never rise from what they bore
  那坏人凭啥享安乐　　　　　　The wicked walk and beg for more

  为一口气，为一个理　　　　　For a breath I keep, for the truth I see
  为一场祭，老子走到底　　　　For a soul set free, I walk with dignity

  我要出了门的人，再晚也归家　I wish each soul returns, in peace, away from harm
  我要天上落的雨，又回到天上　I wish the rain ascends to where it once had come
  我要吃这一口饭、守这一野荒　I wish to keep this vow, and hold what's just in sum
  我要这人间所有道都在青天下　I wish a rightful world where no truth hides in dark

  嫩草怕霜霜怕日　　　　　　　The tender grass will shrink from hoar
  恶人还需犟人磨　　　　　　　But brutes must grind where brutes restore
  我是你杀生得来的报啊　　　　I am the debt your blade once score
  也是你重生的因果　　　　　　And I'm the seed you can't ignore

  我要凿过井的人兜里有盐巴　　I want the working hard one yet not to be so scared
  我要那亏过心的鬼目中有王法　I want the sinful one to pay for whole of the part
  ```

### 如愿｜As You Wished <a name="ruyuan"></a>

  ```
  你是遥遥的路，山野大霧裡的燈　You are the distant road, the light in mountain haze
  我是孩童啊、走在你的眼眸　　　I am a child alone, to walk within your gaze
  你是明月清風，我是你照拂的夢　You are the moon and breeze, I am your sleeping song
  見與不見、都一生與你相擁　　　In shared time we meet, to the life we belong

  而我將愛你所愛的人間　　　　　I'll love the world your love began
  願你所願的笑顏　　　　　　　　And smile the smiles you once had then
  你的手我蹣跚在牽　　　　　　　Your trembling hand, I'll hold it when
  請帶我去明天　　　　　　　　　You lead me on to dawn again
  如果說你曾苦過我的甜　　　　　If you have wept for all my gain
  我願活成你的願　　　　　　　　I'll live to fill your wish so plain
  願不枉啊願勇往啊　　　　　　　Brave and bright, through joy or strain
  這盛世每一天　　　　　　　　　This splendid world shall not be vain

  你是歲月長河，星火燃起的天空　You are the flow of time, the starlight sparks among
  我是仰望者、就把你唱成歌　　　I am the one who looks up and sings you as a song
  你是我之所來，也是我心之所歸　You are the place I'm from, the home my heart has known
  世間所有路、都將與你相逢　　　All roads I walk will turn to meet with you alone

  山河無恙煙火尋常　　　　　　　The land is calm, the echo strong
  可是你如願的跳望　　　　　　　As in the wishes you held so long
  孩子們啊安睡夢鄉　　　　　　　The kids sing a song, and play in lawn
  像你深愛的那樣　　　　　　　　As in the dreams you once had drawn

  而我將夢你所夢的團圓　　　　　I'll dream the dreams you dreamed again
  願你所願的永遠　　　　　　　　And wish the wish that has no end
  走你所走的長路　　　　　　　　I'll walk the path your steps had penned
  這樣的愛你啊　　　　　　　　　And love you thus, my dearest friend
  我也將見你未見的世界　　　　　I'll see the world as it would trend
  寫你未寫的詩篇　　　　　　　　And write the verse you left to mend
  ```
